# IronPath v5.6 Graphics System

The graphics system is intentionally restrained: dark surfaces, electric-blue line graphics, and one consistent dumbbell/IP brand mark. Graphics support navigation and meaning rather than acting as decorative artwork.

- Brand mark: Today, Settings, desktop rail, Home Screen icon
- Activity graphics: Cardio activity selection and progress
- Phase graphics: Four-phase Training Evolution selector
- Achievement graphics: Gains and PR milestones
- Empty states: Workout, History, Weight, and Records
