# IronPath v4 Design Direction

## Visual language

- Near-black foundation
- Deep navy surfaces
- Restrained electric-blue accents
- White primary text and cool-gray secondary text
- Compact information density with clear separation between sections

## Today hierarchy

The dashboard intentionally avoids placing every element inside one large container.

1. **Today’s Workout** — the only major hero card.
2. **Weekly Goals** — three compact progress rings directly on the page background.
3. **Quick Metrics** — four individual cards in a 2 × 2 grid.
4. **Primary Action** — one full-width button beneath the metrics.

## Premium treatment

- Fine blue border accents rather than large color fills
- Stronger title hierarchy and restrained uppercase labels
- Softer panel shadows and deeper spacing
- Small information chips instead of decorative illustrations
- Consistent blue treatment for charts, progress, buttons, navigation, and active states
