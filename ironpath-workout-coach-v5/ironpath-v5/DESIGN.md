# IronPath v5 Design Notes

v5 makes the adaptive programming visible instead of hiding it behind the workout generator.

## Training Evolution

The four phases are shown as compact selectable tabs. A user can inspect every planned phase without changing the active plan. Expanded workouts reveal the planned exercise order, sets, repetitions, rest, and rotation markers.

The current-phase review uses a compact 2×2 metric grid and explicit controls for advancing, repeating, or regenerating the phase.

## Workout names

Workout titles are distinctive enough to be memorable while still describing the session's purpose. They avoid generic A/B labels and overly theatrical fitness-class language.

## Daily motivation

The Today screen displays one short quote selected deterministically from the date. It does not change every time the app opens and does not require an internet connection.
