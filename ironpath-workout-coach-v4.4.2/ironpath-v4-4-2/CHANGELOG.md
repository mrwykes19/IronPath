# IronPath v4.4.2

## Progressive four-phase training blocks

- New and migrated users begin at the first phase of a four-phase training block.
- The first phase uses lighter weights and higher repetition targets.
- Each seven-day phase increases the planned load by one equipment increment.
- The final phase uses the heaviest planned load and a lower repetition range.
- After the fourth phase, the progression returns to the first phase of a newly generated adaptive block.
- Training phases are stored from the user's actual start date rather than tied to the calendar week number.

## Workout identity

- Replaced generic split names with more memorable workout names:
  - Power Lines
  - Ground Force
  - High Tension
  - Load Bearing
  - Foundation Day
  - Total Drive
  - Backbone Session
  - Full Spectrum
  - Forward Press
  - Counterweight

## Preview editing

- The Today workout preview now has an Edit mode.
- Exercises can be reordered, swapped, removed, or added before the workout starts.
- Working sets can be increased or reduced from the preview.
- Preview load recommendations reflect the current training phase.
- Edits are saved into the current planned workout and used when the workout starts.
