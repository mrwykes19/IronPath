# IronPath v5.5 Design Notes

The app remains a premium dark training interface with restrained blue accents. The new brand mark combines a dumbbell silhouette with the IP identity so it reads clearly as a workout app at Home Screen size.

The Cardio panel now uses a two-mode control:
- **Start timer** for live sessions.
- **Add past cardio** for sessions recorded after the fact.

Manual entry stays compact and phone-first, with paired date/duration and distance/calorie fields.
