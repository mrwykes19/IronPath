# IronPath v6.10

- Strength workouts only now count toward Total Workouts.
- Cardio remains tracked separately through Cardio Days, calories, and active minutes.
- Cardio Days and Daily Active Minutes inputs can be cleared and edited normally, then save on blur.
- Weekly active-minute target is calculated from the daily target multiplied by planned workout days.

## 6.9.0

- Added exact workout-day selection in Settings.
- Weekly preview now shows only selected training days.
- Existing profiles receive sensible default days based on workout frequency.

# IronPath v6.8

- Added daily calorie goals multiplied by planned workout days.
- Redesigned weekly goals with three rings and a color-changing calorie progress bar.

# IronPath v6.7

- Added a Training Split selector with Recommended, Full Body, Upper / Lower, Push / Pull / Legs, Hybrid, Body-Part, and Custom options where appropriate for the selected training frequency.
- Added a Custom Split builder for choosing the focus of each training day.
- Changing the split regenerates future workouts while preserving completed history and any active workout.
- Added a seven-day This Week schedule to Today with planned lifting days, rest/cardio days, and current-day highlighting.
- Each planned day can be tapped to preview exercises, sets, rep ranges, rest, and duration.
- Planned workouts can be started early directly from the weekly preview.
- Added a direct route from the weekly preview to the editable Training Plan in Settings.
- Preserved the v6.6 graphics, cardio, weekly goals, adaptive phases, custom workouts, analytics, and backup features.
